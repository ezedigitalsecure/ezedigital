Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Eze Digital

Important: This Privacy Policy explains how Eze Digital collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data. It applies to our website at ezedigital.co.uk and all services we provide. Please read it carefully before using our services.

1. Who We Are

Eze Digital is an integrated digital services business — cybersecurity, web development, compliance guidance, brand design, and CPD-accredited training — based in the United Kingdom.

Data Controller: Eze Digital
Email: hello@ezedigital.co.uk

2. What Personal Data We Collect

Website visitors:

  • IP address (captured automatically for security monitoring)
  • Browser type and operating system (from server logs)
  • Pages visited and time of visit
  • Referral source (how you found our website)

Contact and enquiry forms:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Business name and details
  • Message content

Training and course participants:

  • Full name and email address
  • Organisation or school details
  • Training records and certification status

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, we rely on these lawful bases:

  • Contract performance — to deliver the services, training, or advice you've requested
  • Legitimate interests — to protect our platforms from fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Consent — for marketing communications, where you've given clear agreement
  • Legal obligation — where we're required to retain records by law

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to enquiries and provide agreed services
  • To manage course bookings and issue CPD certificates
  • To send important service updates and information
  • To monitor and protect our systems against unauthorised access
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

5. Who We Share Data With

We never sell your personal data. We share it only where necessary to deliver our services:

  • Website hosting providers — to serve our pages securely
  • Form processing services — to receive and manage your enquiries
  • Training platforms — where required to deliver course materials and certification

All third-party processors are bound by data protection agreements and required to handle your data securely.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Server logs — retained for 90 days then permanently deleted
  • Enquiry records — retained for 2 years or until the relationship is complete
  • Training and certification records — retained as required by CPD standards and tax regulations
  • Marketing preferences — retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to stop

7. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Ask us to delete your data — the "right to be forgotten" — where there's no compelling reason to keep it
  • Restrict how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time — this doesn't affect processing done before withdrawal

To exercise any right, contact us at hello@ezedigital.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

Website: ico.org.uk
Phone: 0303 123 1113

We'd appreciate the chance to address your concerns first — please contact us before raising with the ICO.

9. Data Security

All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Access to client data is restricted to authorised personnel only. We regularly review and update our security practices.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be communicated via our website or direct notice where appropriate.
