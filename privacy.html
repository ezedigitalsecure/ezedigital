Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Eze Digital

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under UK data protection law.

1. Who We Are

Eze Digital is a digital services business based in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for all personal data we process.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Name, email address, contact details
  • Project requirements and communications
  • Billing and payment information
  • Training registration details

Automatically collected data:

  • IP address, browser type, device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on our site

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and manage our services and training
  • Communicate about projects, quotes, and bookings
  • Process payments and issue invoices
  • Send essential service updates and information
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Contract performance — to deliver agreed services
  • Consent — where you have opted in to communications
  • Legitimate interests — to run our business securely and effectively
  • Legal obligation — to comply with accounting and tax requirements

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers essential to delivering our work
  • Professional advisors (accountants, lawyers) as required
  • Law enforcement — only when legally required

6. Data Security

We apply industry-standard security measures. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed:

  • Active client data — retained for the duration of our relationship plus 7 years for legal/accounting purposes
  • Marketing preferences — retained until you unsubscribe
  • Enquiry data — retained for up to 12 months if not converted to a project

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure — where no legal retention requirement applies
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO — the UK data protection regulator
