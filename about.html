Cybersecurity Lead

How We Work Together

Our partnership is built on clear, complementary roles. I lead the business, the education, the client relationships, and the programme design — and Praisegod brings the advanced cybersecurity expertise that gives our work its technical weight and credibility.

We are equals in the business, but we each own our areas of strength. That means you get consistency, clarity, and confidence — knowing your education comes from an experienced, accredited lead, and your technical standards come from a qualified cybersecurity specialist.
