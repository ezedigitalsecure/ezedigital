Privacy & Data Protection

At Eze Digital, privacy is not an afterthought — it is built into how we operate. We comply fully with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What We Collect

When you contact us, we collect only your name, email address, and the message you choose to share. We collect nothing else. No analytics. No tracking cookies. No fingerprinting.

Why We Collect It

Purely to reply to you. We do not use it for marketing. We do not sell it. We do not share it. We hold it only as long as needed to respond to your enquiry, then delete it.

Where It Lives

Data is transmitted over TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS) and processed via Formspree — a UK-compliant data processor. No personal data is stored permanently on our servers.

Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data, correction, or permanent erasure at any time — simply email us your request and we will respond within 30 days.
