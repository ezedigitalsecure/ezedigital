Important — How Access Is Protected

This page uses a password gate to keep the course list tidy and private. For real security of the PDF files themselves, your course files are stored in separate password-protected folders on the server.

  • Each course's files live in their own folder with their own password — a student on one course cannot access another course's files.
  • Even if someone guesses a direct PDF link, the server will still ask for that course's credentials before serving the file.
  • Passwords are issued by Eze Digital on enrolment and can be changed at any time — just get in touch.

Lost your password? Contact Eze Digital via the contact page and we'll re-issue your course access details after verifying your enrolment.
